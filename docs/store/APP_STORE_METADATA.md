## Full Description (4000 characters max)

```
The Nineteenth is the easiest way to organise social golf competitions with your mates. Whether you're running a weekend golf league, a corporate event, or just want to add some friendly competition to your regular rounds - we've got you covered.

EASY COMPETITION SETUP
Create a competition in minutes. Set your game type, add players, and you're ready to go. No complex club systems or confusing interfaces - just simple, social golf.

SCORE ON THE COURSE
Enter scores for your entire group from one device. Works offline so you never lose a score, even in the middle of the course. Large buttons and simple interface designed for on-course use.

AUTOMATIC HANDICAP SCORING
Let us handle the maths. Enter gross scores and we'll calculate net scores, Stableford points, and handicap adjustments automatically. Supports Australian handicap system.

LIVE LEADERBOARDS
See where you stand in real-time. Watch the leaderboard update as scores come in. Compare your performance across rounds and track your progress over the season.

INVITE YOUR MATES
Share a simple code to invite players to your competition. No complicated sign-up process - just join and play.

GAME TYPES
- Stableford (individual & team)
- Stroke Play
- Match Play
- Ambrose (2 & 4 person teams)
- Best Ball

MORE FEATURES
- Team competitions with auto-balanced groups
- Designated scoring pairs for competitive rounds
- Player statistics and performance tracking
- Friends system with head-to-head comparisons
- Australian golf course database
- Dark mode support

DESIGNED FOR AUSTRALIA
Built for Australian golfers, with local courses, Australian handicap calculations, and date formats that make sense.

PERFECT FOR
- Weekend golf groups
- Work golf leagues
- Golf club social committees
- Corporate golf days
- Charity events
- Mates' annual golf trips

Start your competition today and find out who really is the best golfer in your group!

---

Questions or feedback? Contact us at the.nineteenth.aus@gmail.com
```
