Scoring & scorecard — polished

Six round-scoring screens, refined to match the rest of the app — same lime brand, calmer density, clearer score hierarchy. The four score-entry screens — Score entry, Match play, Quick entry and Team match play — are fully live (tap +/−, Pick up, Par, hole arrows) and each carries the GPS distance-to-pin strip. Review scorecard and View round switch tabs, and View round's scorecard offers strip / front-back / full-list views. Flip the Dark tweak to check both themes.
