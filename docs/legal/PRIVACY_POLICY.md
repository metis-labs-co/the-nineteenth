## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information You Provide

When you register for an account or use our App, you may provide us with:

| Information Type | Purpose | Required |
|-----------------|---------|----------|
| **Email Address** | Account creation, login, notifications | Yes |
| **Name** | Display name within competitions | Yes |
| **Profile Photo** | Profile display to other users | No |
| **Golf Handicap (Handicap Index)** | Score calculations and competition features | Yes |
| **Gender** | Used only to apply the correct course rating / slope when calculating your daily (course) handicap | No |
| **Phone Number** | Profile contact detail | No |
| **Home Club** | Profile display and course suggestions | No |
| **Golf ID** (e.g. national golf body membership number) | Profile display; entered by you, not verified with or sent to any golf body | No |
| **Country** | Taken from your device region at sign-up; used for localisation (e.g. date formats, course search) | Automatic |
| **Age band** | Result of the age check at sign-up (under 13 / 13 and over / unknown) and how it was determined. **Your date of birth is never stored** - if you enter it, it is used on your device only to work out the age band | Yes |
| **Scores and round data** | Hole-by-hole scores, putts, fairways, greens, penalties, competition and round participation | Yes (to use scoring) |
| **Content you post** | Comments, likes/reactions, friend requests, and any reports you submit about other content | No |
| **Support requests** | Your name, email address and message when you contact us through the App | No |

### 2.1A Information We Calculate

From your scores we calculate and store:
- **Score differentials** for each eligible round (World Handicap System formula), stored with your scorecards
- A **Social Handicap Index** (based on the best differentials from your last 20 rounds). This is an in-app figure and is **not** an official WHS Handicap Index
- The daily (course) handicap used for each round, plus statistics and achievements

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, we may automatically collect:

- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data**: Features used, screens viewed, time spent in app
- **Log Data**: Error logs, crash reports, performance data

### 2.3 Location Information

If you grant permission, we use your device's location to:
- Help you find nearby golf courses
- Show distances to the green and hazards on the hole map while you play

For these purposes your location is processed on your device and is not stored on our servers.

**Shot tracking (stored on our servers):** if you use the shot-tracking / shot-map feature, the App records the GPS position (latitude and longitude) of each shot you log, together with the round, hole and shot number. These shot positions **are stored on our servers** so you can review your shot maps later. They are linked to your account, included in your data export, and deleted when the round or your account is deleted.

**Custom tee positions (stored on our servers):** if you place a custom tee marker on a hole map, the GPS position of that marker is stored on our servers against your account so it appears next time you play that course. Only you can see your custom tees.

You can disable location access at any time through your device settings; shot tracking and on-course distances will then be unavailable.

### 2.4 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from:
- **Competition Organisers**: If you're added to a competition by an organiser
- **Other Players**: When they score your round or add you to a group

## 5. Data Storage and Security

### 5.1 Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers provided by Supabase, hosted on Amazon Web Services in **Singapore** (ap-southeast-1). This means your personal information is disclosed to, and stored by, an overseas recipient - see Section 8. We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information.

### 5.2 Offline Data

The App stores certain data locally on your device to enable offline functionality:
- Competition details you've accessed
- Scores you've entered (until synced)
- Course information

This local data is stored securely on your device and syncs with our servers when you have an internet connection.

### 5.3 Security Measures

We employ industry-standard security measures including:
- Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL)
- Encryption of data at rest
- Secure authentication protocols
- Regular security assessments
- Access controls and authentication for our systems

### 5.4 Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

**Backups and logs:** our hosting provider keeps automated database backups and server/function logs for a limited period (typically up to 30 days, depending on our hosting plan). Deleted data may remain in these backups and logs until they expire; we only restore from backup to recover from a system failure, and if that happens we re-apply any account deletions. Logs may contain your user ID, IP address and request metadata.

**Anonymised records:** historical scores from competitions you took part in are kept in anonymised form (no longer linked to you) so other players' leaderboards remain correct.

### 6.5 Australian Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of Australia, you have rights under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. You may:
- Request access to your personal information
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Make a complaint about a breach of the Australian Privacy Principles

**How to make a privacy complaint (Australia):**
1. Email your complaint to support@thenineteenth.golf with the subject "Privacy complaint", describing what happened and how you would like it resolved.
2. We will acknowledge your complaint within 7 days and aim to investigate and give you a written response within **30 days**.
3. If you are not satisfied with our response, or we have not responded within 30 days, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC): https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints, phone 1300 363 992.

### 6.6 EU/EEA Privacy Rights

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **Right of Access** (Article 15): You have the right to obtain confirmation as to whether your personal data is being processed and, if so, to access that data along with information about the processing.
- **Right to Rectification** (Article 16): You have the right to have inaccurate personal data corrected and incomplete data completed.
- **Right to Erasure** (Article 17): You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data where it is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected, or where you withdraw consent.
- **Right to Restriction of Processing** (Article 18): You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances, such as when you contest the accuracy of the data.
- **Right to Data Portability** (Article 20): You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format, and to transmit that data to another controller.
- **Right to Object** (Article 21): You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data where processing is based on legitimate interests, including profiling.
- **Right to Lodge a Complaint**: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority in the EU/EEA Member State of your habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@thenineteenth.golf. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

If you are in Australia, using the App means your personal information is disclosed to overseas recipients: principally our hosting provider in **Singapore**, and the service providers in the **United States** listed in Section 4.2. Before disclosing personal information overseas we take reasonable steps to ensure those recipients handle it consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles, including by using providers that contractually commit to data protection and security obligations. We remain accountable under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) for how these recipients handle your personal information.

### 8.1 Transfers from the EU/EEA

Singapore and Australia are not covered by a general European Commission adequacy decision. For transfers to Singapore, Australia and the United States we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914) or, for US recipients certified under it, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

### 8.2 Transfers from the United Kingdom

For transfers from the United Kingdom to Singapore, Australia and the United States we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or, for certified US recipients, the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

### 8.3 Safeguards

Regardless of where your data is processed, we ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place, including contractual obligations on our service providers to protect your personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws.
