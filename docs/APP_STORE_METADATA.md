### Full Description (4000 characters max)

```
The Nineteenth is the easiest way to organise social golf competitions with your mates. Whether you're running a weekend fourball, a monthly medal, or a season-long league, The Nineteenth handles all the admin so you can focus on your game.

SIMPLE COMPETITION SETUP
Create a competition in minutes with our intuitive 4-step wizard. Set the format, add your players, and you're ready to go. No complicated configuration or technical knowledge required.

FLEXIBLE SCORING FORMATS
• Stableford - The classic format with points for each hole
• Stroke Play - Traditional medal play counting every stroke
• Match Play - Head-to-head hole-by-hole competition
• Team formats - Ambrose, Best Ball, and more coming soon

SMART PLAYER MANAGEMENT
• Add players manually or invite them via a unique code
• Track handicaps and update them automatically
• Organise players into groups with automatic pairing suggestions
• Designate scoring pairs for competitive rounds

ON-COURSE SCORING THAT JUST WORKS
• Large, thumb-friendly buttons designed for use on the course
• Score your entire group from one device
• Works offline - no mobile signal required on the course
• Automatic sync when you're back online
• Real-time net score calculations based on handicaps

LIVE LEADERBOARDS
• See standings update in real-time
• Track your position throughout the round
• Compare scores across rounds
• View detailed statistics and trends

AUSTRALIAN GOLF COURSE DATABASE
• Search thousands of Australian golf courses
• Automatic hole data import (par, stroke index)
• Manual course entry for private or new courses
• Support for multiple tee options

BUILT FOR SOCIAL GOLF
• Private competitions - invite only via unique code
• No social media accounts required
• Your data stays private
• Perfect for work golf days, social groups, or club events

DESIGNED IN AUSTRALIA
Built by golfers, for golfers. The Nineteenth uses Australian date formats, supports Golf Australia handicap systems, and understands the unique needs of Australian social golf.

Whether you're organising a one-off corporate golf day or running a year-long competition with your regular group, The Nineteenth makes it easy to keep score, track standings, and crown a champion.

Download now and get your competition started in minutes.
```
