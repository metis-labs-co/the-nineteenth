## FAQ Section

### Q: Do all players need to download the app?
**A:** No! Only the person scoring needs the app. Other players can view the leaderboard via a web link, or download the app if they want to score their own matches.

### Q: Does it work offline?
**A:** Yes. Scoring works completely offline. Your scores save locally and sync automatically when you have connection again.

### Q: What scoring formats do you support?
**A:** Stableford (free tier), plus Stroke Play and Match Play on Social tier. Premium adds team formats like Ambrose, Best Ball, and Scramble.

### Q: How are handicaps handled?
**A:** You enter each player's handicap when adding them. The app automatically calculates strokes received per hole based on the course stroke index, then works out net scores and Stableford points.

### Q: Can I run multiple rounds in one competition?
**A:** Yes! Free tier supports 2 rounds, Social supports 5, and Premium supports 10 rounds per competition.

### Q: Is my data private?
**A:** Absolutely. All competitions are private by default. Only players you invite can see your competition. We don't share or sell your data.

### Q: What if I make a scoring mistake?
**A:** You can edit scores anytime before final submission. After submission, competition admins can make corrections.
