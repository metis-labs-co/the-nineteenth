### Promotional Text (170 characters max, can be updated anytime)

### Description (4000 characters max)

**The Nineteenth** makes running social golf competitions dead simple. Whether it's a monthly comp with your regular four, a season-long league with 20 mates, or a corporate golf day, we've got you sorted.

**CREATE COMPETITIONS IN MINUTES**
No complicated setup. Name your competition, pick the dates, add your players, share the invite code. Your first comp can be live in under 5 minutes.

**SMART PLAYER PAIRING**
Our algorithm pairs players fairly across rounds—rotating partners, balancing handicaps, and making sure everyone plays with everyone. No more arguments about who's playing with who.

**SCORE ON-COURSE, OFFLINE**
Enter scores hole-by-hole with large, thumb-friendly buttons. One phone scores the entire group—no need for everyone to be on their phones. Works completely offline and syncs when you're back in range.

**AUTOMATIC HANDICAP CALCULATIONS**
Enter gross scores, we handle the maths. Strokes received per hole, net scores, and Stableford points are calculated automatically based on handicaps and stroke index.

**LIVE LEADERBOARDS**
Watch the standings update in real-time as groups submit scores. Check from the clubhouse or follow along at home.

**FORMATS FOR EVERY GAME**
• Stableford (Free)
• Stroke Play (Social tier)
• Match Play (Social tier)
• Ambrose, Best Ball, Scramble (Premium tier)

**BUILT FOR AUSTRALIAN GOLFERS**
Designed with the World Handicap System (WHS) in mind. Australian date formats, local terminology, and privacy-first approach.

**FREE TO START**
Create up to 3 competitions with 8 players each, completely free. Upgrade to Social or Premium when you need more players, more rounds, or more formats.

**KEY FEATURES**
✓ 4-step competition setup
✓ Automatic player pairing
✓ Offline scorecard entry
✓ One-device group scoring
✓ Live leaderboard updates
✓ Handicap & net score calculations
✓ Invite players via code
✓ Multiple scoring formats
✓ Dark mode support
✓ Privacy-focused (no data selling)

Download The Nineteenth and have your next competition set up before tee time.
