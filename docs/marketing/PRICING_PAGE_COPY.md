## FAQ Section

### General Questions

**Q: Can I try The Nineteenth before paying?**
A: Absolutely. Our Free tier is fully functional—not a trial. Run up to 3 competitions with 8 players each, no credit card required, no time limit.

**Q: What happens if I go over my tier limits?**
A: You'll see a friendly prompt to upgrade. We won't cut you off mid-competition. Finish what you've started, then upgrade or remove competitions before creating new ones.

**Q: Can I change plans anytime?**
A: Yes. Upgrade instantly, downgrade at the end of your billing period. No lock-in contracts.

**Q: Is there a yearly discount?**
A: Yes! Pay annually and save [XX]% on Social and Premium tiers.

### Downgrade & Cancellation

**Q: What happens to my data if I downgrade?**
A: You keep access to all existing competitions, even if they exceed your new tier limits. You just can't create new ones until you're within limits. We call this "grandfathering"—we won't lock you out of your own data.

**Q: Can I cancel anytime?**
A: Yes. Cancel anytime from your account settings. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

**Q: Do you offer refunds?**
A: If you're not happy within the first 14 days, contact us for a full refund. After that, we don't prorate, but you keep access for the remainder of your billing period.

### Features

**Q: Do all players need to pay?**
A: No! Only organizers need a paid tier if they want premium features. Players can join competitions for free and view leaderboards without paying.

**Q: Is offline scoring included in all plans?**
A: Yes. Offline scorecard entry is available on every tier, including Free. It's core to what we do.

**Q: What's the difference between Social and Premium formats?**
A: Social adds Stroke Play and Match Play formats, plus detailed statistics, handicap history, achievement leaderboards, AI competition creation, guest management, and GPS distance. Premium adds team formats (Ambrose, Best Ball, Scramble), advanced analytics, and side-games (Skins, Wolf, Prize pools).
