## Full Description (4000 characters max)

```
The Nineteenth is the ultimate app for organizing social golf competitions with friends. Whether you're running a weekly group game, a corporate tournament, or a casual weekend showdown, The Nineteenth makes it simple.

KEY FEATURES:

• Create Competitions in Minutes
Set up multi-round competitions with customizable formats. Add players, set handicaps, and generate invite codes for easy joining.

• Score On-Course with Offline Support
Enter scores for your entire group hole-by-hole, even without internet. Scores sync automatically when you're back online.

• Smart Handicap-Based Scoring
Automatic net score calculations for Stableford, Stroke Play, Match Play, and team formats like Best Ball and Scramble.

• Live Leaderboards
Watch standings update in real-time as scores come in. See who's leading and track your position throughout the round.

• Team Competitions
Create teams manually or let the app auto-generate balanced groups. Support for Best Ball, Scramble, and Team Match Play.

• Designated Scoring Pairs
For competitive rounds, assign specific players to score each other for added accountability.

• Player Statistics
Track your performance over time with detailed statistics including scoring averages, trends, and recent rounds.

• Friends & Social
Add friends, compare stats head-to-head, and see how you stack up against your golf buddies.

• Australian Golf Course Database
Access thousands of Australian golf courses with hole-by-hole data including par, stroke index, and yardages.

Perfect for:
- Social golf groups
- Weekend warriors
- Corporate golf events
- Golf club social committees
- Anyone who wants to add friendly competition to their golf

Download The Nineteenth and take your social golf game to the next level!
```
