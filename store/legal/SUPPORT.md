## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**How do I create an account?**
Download The Nineteenth from the App Store or Google Play, then sign up with your email address. You'll receive a verification link to complete registration.

**How do I set my handicap?**
During onboarding, you'll be asked to enter your handicap. You can update it anytime in Profile > Edit Profile.

**Can I use the app without an account?**
No, an account is required to create and join competitions, track scores, and access all features.

---

### Competitions

**How do I create a competition?**
1. Tap the "+" button on the home screen
2. Enter competition details (name, dates, format)
3. Add rounds and select courses
4. Share the invite code with players

**How do I join a competition?**
1. Get an invite code from the organizer
2. Tap "Join Competition" on the home screen
3. Enter the code and confirm

**What game types are supported?**
- Stableford
- Stroke Play
- Match Play
- Best Ball (team)
- Scramble (team)

---

### Scoring

**How do I enter scores?**
1. Open the round you're playing
2. Tap "Start Scoring"
3. Enter strokes for each hole
4. Submit when complete

**Can I score for other players in my group?**
Yes! When scoring, you can enter scores for all players in your group.

**Does the app work offline?**
Yes, you can enter scores without internet connection. Scores will sync automatically when you're back online.

**How are net scores calculated?**
Net scores are calculated using your handicap and the course stroke index. The app automatically applies strokes received per hole.

---

### Account & Settings

**How do I change my email/password?**
Go to Profile > Settings > Account to update your credentials.

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to Profile > Settings > Delete Account. This action is permanent and will remove all your data.

**How do I enable dark mode?**
Go to Profile > Settings > Appearance and select your preferred theme (Light, Dark, or System).

---

### Subscriptions

**What's included in the free tier?**
- 3 competitions
- 2 rounds per competition
- 10 friends
- Stableford scoring only

**How do I upgrade my subscription?**
Go to Profile > Subscription to view plans and upgrade.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). Go to your device's subscription settings to cancel.

---

### Troubleshooting

**The app won't sync my scores**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Pull down to refresh the screen
3. Close and reopen the app
4. If the issue persists, contact support

**I can't find a golf course**
Our database covers Australian courses. If a course is missing, you can add it manually or contact us to request it be added.

**The leaderboard isn't updating**
1. Pull down to refresh
2. Check that all players have submitted their scorecards
3. Ensure you have an internet connection

**I forgot my password**
On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password" and enter your email. We'll send you a reset link.
