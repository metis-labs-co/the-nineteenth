Course Statistics Screen — Tab Structure

How should we organize the course stats into tabs?

A

Two Tabs: Overview | Holes

Overview tab: Score trend chart, overview stats (times played, avg, best, worst), score distribution, averages, par type stats, advanced stats (driving, approach, short game, putting, bunkers, hazards), recent rounds.

Holes tab: Horizontal scrollable hole selector (1-18 pill/card strip at top), tapping a hole shows its breakdown below — score trend sparkline, score distribution bars, avg putts, GIR%, fairway%, best/worst.

Simple split. Overview is the "course summary", Holes is the "drill into each hole" view. Fewer tabs = less navigation overhead.

B

Three Tabs: Overview | Holes | Game Stats

Overview tab: Score trend chart, overview stats, score distribution, averages, recent rounds.

Holes tab: Horizontal hole selector strip, per-hole breakdown below.

Game Stats tab: Advanced stats only — driving (FIR% + miss diagram), approach (GIR% + miss diagram), short game (scrambling), putting (depth stats), bunkers, hazards. All scoped to this course.

Mirrors the 3-tab pattern from My Statistics screen (Overview / Scoring / Game Stats). Keeps advanced stats separate so Overview stays clean.

C

Two Tabs: Overview | Holes (with per-hole advanced stats)

Overview tab: Score trend chart, overview stats, score distribution, averages, par type stats, course-level advanced stats (driving, approach, putting, bunkers, hazards), recent rounds.

Holes tab: Horizontal hole selector strip, per-hole breakdown below — includes not just basics (score distribution, avg putts) but also per-hole fairway miss direction and GIR% where data exists.

Everything accessible in just two tabs, but the Holes tab is richer with advanced per-hole data. The Overview becomes the "course-wide" view, Holes becomes "hole-specific" with all relevant detail inline.
