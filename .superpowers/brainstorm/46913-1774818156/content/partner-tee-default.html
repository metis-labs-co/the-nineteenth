Partner Tee Defaults

When a partner is added to the round, what's their initial tee?

A

Default to round creator's tee (Recommended)

You pick your tee first (shown at top of the list). Each partner added inherits your tee selection. They can override individually.

Covers the common case (everyone on same tee) with zero extra taps. Different-tee players just tap to change.

B

Start blank — everyone picks

No default tee for anyone. Each player (including you) must explicitly select a tee before proceeding.

Forces conscious tee selection. More taps but no accidental wrong-tee situations. Could be annoying when everyone plays the same tee.

C

Tee optional — don't require it

Tee picker shown but not mandatory. If no tee selected, handicap calculations just use the player's base handicap (no daily HC).

Least friction, but means some players might not get accurate daily handicaps. Good for casual rounds where tee data might not exist.
